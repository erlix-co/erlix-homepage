Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1,

This Privacy Policy explains how Erlix ("we", "our", "us") handles information when you use our website, apps, and related tools for organizational communication, productivity, and community utility services.

It applies to the Erlix website and published tools, including LinkCheck (link and message safety analysis), BCC Alert (Gmail and Outlook productivity add-ons), and Community SMS (Android). Some sections apply only where a feature exists (for example SMS permissions on Android).

1. Introduction

Erlix provides software tools for schools, communities, organizations, and teams. Our tools are designed to support clear and responsible group communication workflows.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the tool, we may process:

  • User-provided files (for example CSV, Excel, or exported spreadsheet files)
  • User-entered message content and communication templates
  • Technical operational logs for service reliability and security monitoring
  • Basic device/app diagnostics where required to operate the app safely

Examples by product (non-exhaustive):

  • LinkCheck: text or URLs you submit for analysis are sent to our servers only to perform the check you requested and return results.
  • BCC Alert: operates within your email client environment to help prevent mistaken group addressing; it is not designed to collect unrelated mailbox content on Erlix servers.
  • Community SMS: list and message data are processed primarily on your device for composing and sending messages you approve.

We do not sell personal data.

3. Local File Processing

Erlix tools primarily process imported files locally on the user's device. Imported CSV/Excel/Google Sheets-export files are used only for user-requested processing steps.

We do not secretly upload imported contact files to our servers.

4. SMS Permissions

Some Android tools may request SMS-related permissions (for example SEND_SMS) only to enable user-initiated messaging features.

  • Messages are sent only after explicit user action and approval.
  • No background mass sending is performed without user initiation.
  • Community SMS features are intended for organizational communication, school notifications, and community group communication.

5. Google Drive Integration

Where Google Drive file picker or import is available, access is used only to open files that the user explicitly selects. Erlix does not use Google Drive access to browse unrelated user data.

6. Data Storage

Most processing data remains on-device for the requested workflow. If limited operational logs are stored on our infrastructure, they are used for reliability, abuse prevention, and product improvement.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as hosting, security, analytics, or abuse detection providers) to operate our products. Such providers process data only as needed for those functions and under their own applicable terms and policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse. No system is perfectly secure, but we continuously improve our controls.

9. User Control

Users control what they import, process, and send. You may delete local files and app data at any time through your device or app settings. You are responsible for lawful and appropriate communication use in your jurisdiction.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate legal basis.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
